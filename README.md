### Hi, I'm Zak.

I'm a molecular biologist who creates things. My master's work tracked how skin microbes change after death (930 samples, about 20 million 16S reads, and a machine-learning model of time since placement). Since then I've run GLP studies for medical-device companies, managed a COVID testing site, and founded [Sploosh](https://gosploosh.com), a hardware company.

These days I prototype my own tools with Claude Code. This is where the ones worth sharing live.

**What's here**

- [spec-scout](https://github.com/Zak-Does-Science/spec-scout): turns a requirements list into a shortlist of compliant models and a priced cost sheet, with a screenshot behind every price for procurement. A standalone rebuild of a Claude add-on I made at work.
- [foundry-campaign-modules](https://github.com/Zak-Does-Science/foundry-campaign-modules): Foundry VTT modules for my D&D campaign. Players walk 8-bit malls full of NPCs and hidden secrets, unlock a lore wiki through research rolls, and scroll the world's own social media. GIFs in the README.
- [talespire-crowd-placer](https://github.com/Zak-Does-Science/talespire-crowd-placer): a C# plugin that drops a crowd of named characters onto a TaleSpire board from a JSON list. Built because nothing else could.

More to come, including small science tools.
